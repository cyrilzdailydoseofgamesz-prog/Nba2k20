NBA 2K20 ROS Maker

Opens a version-5 ROS, decrypts and unpacks it, lets you edit the roster data, then rebuilds a correctly encrypted ROS with fresh checksums. Player records are shown as raw bytes (their layout isn't mapped yet).

Update to current NBA rosters

Matches today's NBA players (from ESPN, needs internet) to the 2K20 rows by name, then moves players to their current teams and updates jersey numbers. Rookies and new players can't be added yet. Experimental: test in the game.
